What it does

A complete dispatch simulation built as a C++ object-oriented programming project, with a Qt desktop interface.

Four incident types

Medical, fire, road accident and crime incidents, each with its own priority and vehicle needs.

Smart vehicle selection

Only free, suitable vehicles are considered. The fastest estimated arrival wins, and vehicle speed is taken into account.

Route planning

Fastest routes are found with Dijkstra's algorithm. Add traffic or close roads and new dispatches adapt.

Interactive city map

Zoom, pan and click places on the map. Watch vehicles drive to incidents and return to base.

Prepared scenarios

Normal traffic, rush hour, blocked road and simultaneous incidents, played step by step or run at once.

Statistics and saved runs

Response and waiting times, incidents resolved, and runs saved to a local database to review later.

How it works

Three steps from a report to a result.

Report an incident

Pick a place on the map, the type of emergency and its priority.

The simulator dispatches

It chooses the best available vehicle, plans the route and shows the reason for its choice.

Watch and review

Time advances minute by minute. Resolve incidents, then open the statistics and event log.

Install in a minute

No account and no setup. Download, open, and the simulator starts.

  • Click Download for Windows.
  • Open the downloaded ERS-Setup-1.0.0.exe from your browser's downloads.
  • Follow the installer; it can launch the simulator when it finishes.
  • If Windows shows a "Windows protected your PC" notice, choose More info, then Run anyway. This student project is not code-signed.
Educational simulation only. It uses a fictional city and synthetic data and must not be used for real emergency decisions.
